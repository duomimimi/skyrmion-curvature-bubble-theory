## Abstract | 摘要
Traditional Alcubierre warp drive suffers from extreme energy requirement and requires exotic matter distributed across spacetime.
This work constructs a compact Skyrmion thin-shell curvature bubble model, using Israel junction conditions for the geometric origin of negative energy. The model localizes negative energy on the thin topological shell, drastically reducing the required energy magnitude. Linear perturbation and critical disintegration velocity of the moving thin shell are analyzed.

传统Alcubierre曲率引擎存在能耗极高、需要全域分布奇异物质的难题。本文构建紧致Skyrmion薄壳曲率泡模型，借助Israel接合条件实现负能量的几何起源。该模型将负能量局域在拓扑薄壳上，大幅降低所需能量量级；同时分析运动薄壳的线性扰动与临界解体速度。
